    HOW TO PLAY

    Wipe out every fortress core on the map to clear the stage. Shoot the cannons and walls to light a fuse and chain-blast a route to the core.

    Your ship never stops – steer with the pad, and it keeps flying the way you last pointed. FIRE shoots forwards and backwards at once.

    The world wraps around. Watch the radar and don't touch the fortress walls.

    Extra ship at 2,000 points, then every 3,000.

    Touch pad + FIRE · Keys arrows/WASD, space, P · Pad stick, A, Start

    OPTIONS

    Floating pad appears under your thumb and follows it. Auto-fire shoots continuously (F on keyboard).
